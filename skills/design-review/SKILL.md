# Design Review

Read the [design contract](../design/references/design-contract.md), [principles](../design/references/design-principles.md), [quality](../design/references/quality.md), and relevant [integrations](../design/references/integrations.md). Review is read-only on source.

## Inspect And Report

Establish the task, audience, constraints, and affected scope. Resolve consequential ambiguity through [design-intent](../design-intent/SKILL.md). Inspect the artifact: capture relevant rendered states and exercise available interactions for live interfaces; use [visual evidence](../design-intent/references/visual-evidence.md) for static or incomplete material.

Apply relevant benchmarks and two-book principles to actual choices. Use observation and existing checks where sufficient; new validation follows the Dev integration. Inspect the affected scope without forcing a whole-product audit.

Report material findings with their location, consequence, and scoped recommendation. Distinguish observation from inferred cause; include a next check only when it resolves uncertainty. Apply the shared quality verdict and completion rule.

When fixes are also authorized, read [design-build](../design-build/SKILL.md) and follow its engineering integration before editing.
